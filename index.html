Our Framework

About CarbonAxis & Our Mission

Adhering strictly to global ecological standards to drive absolute project integrity.

Our Role: Sourcing & Distribution Partner

CarbonAxis functions as a trusted liquidity intermediary in the voluntary carbon market. We do not develop projects ourselves. Instead, we perform extensive risk and quality due diligence to procure premium carbon credits in bulk from certified project developers globally, distributing them directly to corporate clients who need high-integrity assets to clear their emissions.

Core Carbon Principles (ICVCM)

CarbonAxis aligns project verification criteria with the Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market's Core Carbon Principles. This ensures additions are permanent, double-counting is mathematically impossible, and social co-benefits are fully verified.

Strict Verification Protocols

Each project in our database goes through continuous monitoring, reporting, and verification (MRV) using modern satellite data and third-party auditors (Verra, Gold Standard, Plan Vivo) to audit and match real-world carbon abatement.
